A few days ago, I was scrolling on the YouTube homepage when this video popped up:

[Why You DON'T probably Need A Computer Science Degree](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SZ90LjoU6eA)

> [...]
> The barrier to learning software development has completely collapsed. 20 years ago, getting high quality technical education outside university was much harder. **Today, bro, we have too much information.** There are university lectures online for free, books, documentation, interactive coding platforms, open-source projects, developer communities like The Odin Project, entire computer science curriculums, and approximately 84 billion YouTube tutorials explaining JavaScript promises.
> [...]
> You can also learn it terribly. That's the actual problem. **Information isn't the bottleneck anymore. Structure is.** A degree gives you that structure. Someone already decided what you should learn first, what comes next, what fundamentals you shouldn't skip, and what increasingly difficult concepts you should eventually work toward.
> [...]

This video fundamentally reshaped my relationship with knowledge.
