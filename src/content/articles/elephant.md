Vietnam is often thought to be part of the Sinosphere. While Sinitic influence permeates much of Vietnamese culture, its foundation was greatly influenced by the Indosphere, where elephants were no less living gods.[^1]

In Vietnamese history, elephants were instruments of warfare:
- The _Trưng sisters_, considered to be a national symbol in Vietnam, are often depicted as two women riding two giant war elephants.[^2]
- There are two pairs of concrete elephant statues kneeling in reverence to the tomb Lê Lợi, who led the Lam Sơn uprising.[^3]
- On the morning of the 5th Tet of Rooster (Jan 1st, 1789), Emperor Quang Trung directly rode the elephants to command the great army to fight straight at Ngọc Hồi.[^4]
