Vietnam is often thought to be part of the Sinosphere. While Sinitic influence permeates much of Vietnamese culture, its foundation was greatly influenced by the Indosphere, where elephants was of supreme importance to it.

In Vietnamese history, elephants were instruments of warfare. The _Trưng sisters_, considered to be a national symbol in Vietnam, are often depicted as two women riding two giant war elephants. [ADD: Lam Sơn Uprising, Battle of Ngọc Hồi - Đống Đa]

Meanwhile, in the Vietnamese legend _Sơn Tinh, Thủy Tinh_, the “nine-tusked elephant” (voi chín ngà) is one of the wedding gifts demanded by King Hùng in his search for a husband for Princess Mỵ Nương.
